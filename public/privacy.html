Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

The short version

  • Dr. Squeeze has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, photos, contacts or location.
  • Your progress is saved on your device only.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use a device advertising ID to show and measure ads, with your consent where the law requires it.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play / Apple. We use RevenueCat to check what you bought, with an anonymous ID.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

What is stored on your device

Coins, levels, unlocked items, settings and game statistics are stored locally using the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app, or Settings → Reset progress, deletes them. We cannot see or recover this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To pay for the free game we show ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use: your device's advertising identifier, IP address (used to estimate your region), device and app information, and ad-interaction data (for example whether an ad was shown or tapped). This is used to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown (Google's consent form). You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. On iPhone, the game asks for tracking permission first; if you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown.

See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card details. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts so the game can unlock what you bought and restore purchases.

Children

Dr. Squeeze is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads in the game are requested as not child-directed. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we don't hold personal information on our own servers, most requests are handled by resetting your advertising ID, using Privacy choices, or contacting Google / RevenueCat directly; we're happy to help.

Security

Data sent to our service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.

Contact

Questions or requests: the developer email address shown on our App Store / Google Play listing. Data controller: the developer of Dr. Squeeze.
